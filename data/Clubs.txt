### Clubs at NIT Andhra Pradesh

---

1. Praayatnam Society  
Faculty In-charge: Dr. Veeresh Kumar G B  
About: Provides career guidance based on students' interests and talents. Prepares students for competitive exams and fosters critical thinking.  
Vision: Universal literacy and belief in unconventional career paths.  
Mission: Improve education quality, reduce dropout rates, and promote school-community relationships.

---

2. Dhwani (Music Club)  
About: Founded by the 2017–20 batch. Encourages musical creativity through events and workshops.  
Vision: Discover and enhance hidden musical talents.  
Mission: Promote musical composition and self-expression through melody, harmony, rhythm, and timbre.

4. Painting and Photography Club (PNP)  
About: Encourages photography and painting as hobbies. Fosters creativity and observation. Organizes art-related events.  
Purpose: Enable expression of thoughts and emotions through art. Build a community of artists and photographers.

---

5. Literary and Debate Club  
About: One of the oldest and most active clubs. Hosts SWEVEN literary fest and regular events to develop communication skills.  
Vision: Develop confidence, creativity, and self-efficacy in students.  
Mission: Organize thought-provoking events and foster socially responsible values.

---

6. Innovation & Entrepreneurship Cell (I&E Cell)  
Founded by: Mr. Kiran Babu Pilla, Mr. Chanakya Rajappagari, and Mr. M J Saura Siddarth  
Faculty Advisor: Dr. V Sudarshana Deepa  
About: Promotes entrepreneurial mindset through events like Startup Expo, TEDxNITAP, Ideathon, etc.  
Vision: Create a self-reliant India through innovation.  
Mission: Support student ideas from conception to realization.

---

7. Shreshta Club  
About: Builds leadership and character by focusing on holistic development (Physical, Emotional, IQ, Spiritual).  
Mission: Conduct seminars on leadership, mind management, self-discovery, and more.  
Credentials: Alumni from IITs, NITs; invited notable speakers.

About: Promotes environmental awareness and values. Conducts campaigns like Swachhata Pakhwada.

- Promote water management and cleanliness  
- Instill morals, ethics, and societal values
