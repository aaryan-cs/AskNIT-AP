 --- GENERAL HOSTEL & MESS INFO ---

Hostel Objectives:
The hostel serves as a home on campus, offering utilities, food, sports, and a hygienic environment. Students must maintain decorum and bring credit to themselves and the institute.

Mess Objectives:
NIT Andhra Pradesh provides one mess for the girls’ hostel and two messes for boys’ hostels, run on a cooperative basis. All boarders must join the mess compulsorily. For issues or emergencies, contact the caretaker first, then the warden.
